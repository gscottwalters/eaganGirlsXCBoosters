Supporting Eagan Girls Cross Country

Through community involvement, volunteer service, and resources, we help every athlete succeed.

Welcome!

The Eagan High School Girls Cross Country Booster Club fosters parent and community involvement to support our athletes and school spirit. We provide volunteer service and financial support to enhance the program through coaching, equipment, transportation, and events. Our goal is to celebrate every athlete’s accomplishments and enrich the overall team experience.
