Supporting Eagan Girls Cross Country

Through community involvement, volunteer service, and resources, we help every athlete succeed.

Welcome!

The Eagan High School Girls Cross Country Booster Club fosters parent and community involvement to support our athletes and school spirit. We provide volunteer service and financial support to enhance the program through coaching, equipment, transportation, and events. Our goal is to celebrate every athlete’s accomplishments and enrich the overall team experience.

Volunteer Opportunities

Our team relies on parent volunteers to keep our runners fueled and celebrated! Please consider signing up to host or contribute:

🍝 Host a Pre-Meet Pasta Meal

Host our 31 wonderful girls in your home, garage, or outdoor space for pre-meet Pasta Feeds! Girls arrive after practice ends and stay for an hour or so. Food and drinks will be provided by the Booster club.

Sign Up for Pasta Meals

🍪 Bring Post-Race Cookies & Snacks

Nothing kicks off the post-meet recovery quite like cookies. By taking a slot, you are committing to bring 2 dozen cookies for our runners to enjoy after each meet.

Sign Up for Post-Race Cookies
