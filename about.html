About the Booster Club

The Eagan High School Girls Cross Country Booster Club supports the team by promoting positive parent and community involvement while enhancing the overall athlete experience. Our goal is to foster school spirit, celebrate individual and team accomplishments, and ensure every runner has the resources needed to succeed.

Through volunteer service and fundraising, the Booster Club provides financial and logistical support for items not covered by the school district or team budget. This support helps strengthen the program and enrich the season for all athletes.

What the Boosters Provide

  • Transportation Support: Funding for team bus transportation to away meets and championship events.
  • Pre-Race Pasta Feeds: Organizing and hosting team pasta dinners before major meets to promote nutrition, team bonding, and pre-race preparation.
  • Supplemental Coaching: Financial support for additional coaching assistance beyond what is provided by the school district.
  • Equipment & Supplies: Purchasing training and meet-day items not covered by the team budget.
  • Team Events & Celebrations: Supporting end-of-season banquets, senior recognition, and team-building activities.

Get Involved

The Booster Club relies on parent and community volunteers to make these efforts possible. Whether helping at pasta feeds, serving on the board, or assisting with fundraising and events, there are many ways to support the team.
