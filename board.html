Booster Club Board Members

The Eagan High School Girls Cross Country Booster Club is led by a dedicated group of parent volunteers who work together to support our athletes and enhance the team experience. Each board member fills an important role in helping the club provide funding, volunteer support, and community involvement.

  • President — Jen Sherman

    Leads the Booster Club, sets agendas, and facilitates monthly board meetings. Provides overall leadership for club activities.

  • Vice President — (Open)

    Supports the President and fills in as needed to help guide club operations.

  • Treasurer — Erica Bryan-Wegner & Bob Frank

    Manages club finances, including budgeting, deposits, and payments. Helps prepare financial reports for the board.

  • Secretary — Sandy Davidson

    Keeps accurate minutes of board meetings and maintains documentation for the Booster Club.

  • Website Director — Scott Walters

    Maintains the Booster Club website and online communications.

  • Fundraising Coordinator — Julie Boerger

    Coordinates fundraising activities to support team needs throughout the season.

  • Directors at Large — Wade Brost, Andy Johnson, Jason Bryan-Wegner

    Serves as a board director helping guide club activities and support functions.

About Board Meetings

Booster Club board meetings are typically held on the third Monday of each month at 7:00 p.m. in Room 135 at Eagan High School (unless otherwise noted). Meetings are open to all parents and Booster Club members. Topics often include voting on funding requests (such as transportation or event support), reviewing upcoming activities, and planning volunteer needs for the season. The club generally does not meet in December or January.

New board members are welcomed — elections are held at the March board meeting, and participation by all families is encouraged.
